# BinFlip

BinFlip builds inventory, listing, profit tracking, and automation tools for eBay sellers.

Our goal is to make managing one or multiple eBay stores simpler, faster, and more reliable, especially for sellers handling large inventories.

## What BinFlip helps with

- Inventory and cost of goods tracking
- Listing management and bulk workflows
- Moving listings between eBay stores
- Promoted Listings automation
- eBay sales and profit tracking
- Multi eBay store management
- Order and shipping workflows

BinFlip is built specifically for eBay sellers and is actively developed based on real seller workflows and feedback.

## See us on eBay

We do not just build software for eBay sellers, we actively sell on eBay ourselves and use BinFlip as part of our own day to day operations.

Running our own store helps us build around real workflows. We deal with the same things other sellers do, including inventory management, listing maintenance, offers, orders, shipping, promoted listings, and keeping track of profitability.
